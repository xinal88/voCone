# VO-cone experiment: pre-registered choices

Fixed on 2026-10-31, before any training or evaluation run. Choices the task leaves to the group
were made here because the group's backbone run and held-out list were not available yet; if
the group supplies them before evaluation starts, theirs replace decisions 2 and 5 and this file
records the switch.

## Fixed by the task

- 16 headings at 2πk/16 counterclockwise from forward, speed = max(measured speed, 0.3 m/s),
  pedestrians within 12 m, contact radius robot + pedestrian, value `1 - t / 5 s`, max over pedestrians.
- Arms: AGENT_5_VO (new), AGENT_5 and AGENT_5_TTC (controls). Reward identical across arms.
- Held-out: pedestrian drivers socialgail and nsp, three scenario families, 15 and 20 pedestrians.
- Readout: zero-contact success first, then success, contacts per episode, time to goal,
  pedestrian deviation; delta against both controls with a paired bootstrap 95% interval per split.
- Verdict: chase / drop / inconclusive on held-out intervals only.

## Decisions

1. **Relative velocity per heading.** `v_ped - speed * heading_k`; pedestrian velocities are absolute
   in the robot frame. The heading of travel reproduces the TTC generator (tested).
2. **Training config.** Arena-Training `sb_training_config.yaml` with: `n_timesteps: 4000000`,
   success early stop off, `seed: 42`, `env_seed: 42`, and the DRL-VO heading reward
   `active_heading_direction` on at its unit defaults, for all arms. Generated by `make_configs.py`;
   the arms differ only in name and architecture.
3. **Training order.** AGENT_5_VO first, then AGENT_5, then AGENT_5_TTC; same sim, robot and config.
   A provisional VO-vs-AGENT_5 readout may be produced before TTC finishes; the verdict waits for both.
4. **One training seed per arm.** Bootstrap intervals cover episode variance only, not seed variance.
5. **Held-out scenario families** (by interaction type, scenarios from `hospital_scenarios`):
   - head-on: `s01_head_on`, `s06_bidirectional_flow`
   - crossing: `s03_perpendicular_crossing`, `s07_crossing_pair`, `s08_crossing_group`
   - confined: `s05_doorway_passage`, `s09_corridor_crowd`, `s10_bottleneck_queue`
   None appears in training (the curriculum uses random tasks only).
6. **Verdict metric.** Zero-contact success (`success` and no pedestrian contact), intervals against
   both controls pooled over held-out splits.
